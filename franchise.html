FRANCHISE FAQ

Questions? Let's Talk.

Have questions about location, setup, courses or the franchise model? Start a conversation with the academy.

Individuals, entrepreneurs, education professionals and organisations interested in establishing a technical education centre can contact the academy to discuss suitability.

Location, available infrastructure, local student demand, staffing, course requirements and operational planning should be discussed before finalising the centre.

Franchise investment and other commercial terms should be confirmed directly with the academy because they may depend on the proposed centre, location and model.

Use the enquiry option on this page or contact the academy directly to discuss your location, background and franchise requirements.
